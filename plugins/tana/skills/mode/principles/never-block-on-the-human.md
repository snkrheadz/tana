# Never block on the human
Open when: you are about to ask "should I do X?" about work that can be undone.

Covered by `../pillars/keep-moving.md`: sort the question, act on reversible choices, ask only about irreversible or preference calls.

Nuance: the person is supervising asynchronously, and changes are reviewable, so a wrong guess usually costs less than waiting. Do the work, show it, give the reason. When you notice a defect on the way, note it and fix it in the next pass. Product direction is theirs; execution is yours.
