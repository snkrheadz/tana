# Cut AI tells

Read the text once for meaning. Then go through the checks below, rewrite, and read it
again. Keep the author's facts and tone. Never add a claim.

## Wording

- Swap showy words for plain ones: "use" for "utilize" and "leverage", "help" for
  "facilitate", "many" for "numerous", "if" for "in the event that", "is" for "serves
  as" or "boasts". Also watch: crucial, pivotal, delve, tapestry, landscape, vibrant,
  testament, underscore, intricate, interplay.
- Delete empty openers and closers: "It is important to note", "I hope this helps",
  "Certainly!", "Great question", and a final line that says the future looks bright.
- Cut hedges stacked on hedges. One "may" is enough.
- Name the source of "experts say" or delete the sentence.
- Drop trailing "-ing" clauses that only gesture ("highlighting its importance").
- Pick one name for a thing and keep it. Do not rotate synonyms.

## Shape

- "Not just X but Y" becomes the plain claim.
- Do not group ideas in threes unless there are three.
- "From A to B" needs A and B on one scale. Otherwise list them.
- A bold label followed by a colon that repeats the line is noise. Write the sentence.
  A bold lead-in that names an item and adds new detail is fine.
- Bold only what a reader must not miss.

## Punctuation and format

- No em dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- A colon introduces a list or an example. It is not a joint between clauses.
- Sentence case headings. Straight quotes. No emoji in headings or bullets.

## Say something

- Replace a feeling with a mechanism or a number. "Fast and reliable" becomes
  "p95 is 40 ms". "Types that follow your schema" becomes "renaming a column fails the
  build". If a sentence could sit unchanged in another project's docs, cut it.
- Replace figures of speech with the literal phrase. Avoid noun metaphors such as
  substrate, vector, bedrock, scaffolding, north star, flywheel, and verbs such as
  "rides along" or "stands on".
- Use the active voice and name the actor: "the loader parses the file".
- Prefer a stronger verb to a verb plus an adverb.
- Split any sentence the reader has to reread. Keep articles and verbs. Spell out arrows
  and abbreviations: "The parser rejects a bad date and exits with code 2".

## Check

Read the result aloud. If a sentence sounds like a press release, rewrite it as you
would say it to a colleague.
