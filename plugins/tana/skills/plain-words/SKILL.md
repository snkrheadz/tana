Say your last message again for someone outside the field.

1. Keep the point. Cut the detail the reader does not need to act on.
2. Replace each term of art with what it means, or drop it.
3. Use short sentences and everyday words, as if talking across a table.
4. If a number or name matters, keep it. If it does not, leave it out.

Do not add new claims or re-do any work. Reply with the rewrite only.
